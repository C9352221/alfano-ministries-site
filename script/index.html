The Soul-Winning Script

It takes about ninety seconds. It costs nothing. It does not require a passport.

This is the script. That is all it is. There is nothing to buy here and nothing to sign up for.

People have used these same words to lead thousands to Jesus, one conversation at a time, in airports and grocery lines and hospital rooms. Not through a ministry or a budget or a stage. Just a person with a question and the willingness to ask it.

Read it a few times until it is yours. Then carry it.

Word for Word

The Script

Step One

The Opening Question

"Has anyone ever told you that God loves you and that He has a wonderful plan for your life? I have a real quick, but important question to ask you. If you were to die this very second, do you know for sure, beyond a shadow of a doubt, that you would go to Heaven?"

If they say yes, ask: "Great, why would you say yes?" If the answer is anything other than "I have Jesus in my heart" or something similar, keep going. If they say no, or "I hope so," keep going.
Step Two

The Good News

"Let me quickly share with you what the Holy Bible reads. It reads 'for all have sinned and come short of the glory of God' and 'for the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.' The Bible also reads, 'For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.' And you're a 'whosoever,' right? Of course you are; all of us are."

Romans 3:23, Romans 6:23, Romans 10:13.
Step Three

Pray for Them, by Name

"I'm going to say a quick prayer for you. Lord, bless [name] and their family with long and healthy lives. Jesus, make Yourself real to them and do a quick work in their heart. If [name] has not received Jesus Christ as their Lord and Savior, I pray they will do so now."

Use their name out loud. It matters more than anything else on this page.
Step Four

The Invitation

"[Name], if you would like to receive the gift that God has for you today, say this after me with your heart and lips out loud."

"Dear Lord Jesus, come into my heart. Forgive me of my sin. Wash me and cleanse me. Set me free. Jesus, thank You that You died for me. I believe that You are risen from the dead and that You're coming back again for me. Fill me with the Holy Spirit. Give me a passion for the lost, a hunger for the things of God and a holy boldness to preach the gospel of Jesus Christ. I'm saved; I'm born again, I'm forgiven and I'm on my way to Heaven because I have Jesus in my heart."
Step Five

Tell Them It Is Done

"All of your sins are forgiven. Always remember to run to God and not from God, because He loves you and has a great plan for your life."

Then get their name and number, and invite them to church. Follow up. A new believer with nobody to call is a new believer alone.

Print It Out

Carry It in Your Pocket

A pocket card with the whole script on both sides. Four cards to a page. Print it, cut them out, keep one on you and give the rest away.

Download the Card (PDF)

Print double-sided, flip on the short edge. Standard letter paper works fine.

  • Keep it simple. You are not selling anything. You are asking a question.
  • Ask anyone. The person next to you on the plane, in line, at the counter. Most people say yes.
  • Do not skip the name. Praying for someone by name changes the room.
  • Four or five a day adds up to more souls in a lifetime than you could count.
  • Start with the people you already know. They are the hardest ones to ask, and they are the ones nobody else will.
