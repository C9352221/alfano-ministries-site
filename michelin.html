The Book

From a Small-Town Deli to the Greatest Kitchens in the World

Chef Marc Alfano came from a small town in western Pennsylvania, where fine dining meant putting French fries on a salad. He enrolled at Le Cordon Bleu in Pittsburgh in 2002, graduated first in his class, and opened his own deli.

In 2008 he worked stages at Restaurant Daniel in New York, Charlie Trotter's in Chicago, and the French Laundry in Napa Valley, three Michelin-rated kitchens in a single year.

This is the map he wishes he'd had. Read it now, act on what you learn, and get to your goals a lot faster than he did. The story happens in the kitchen, but the lessons work in any industry you're passionate about.
