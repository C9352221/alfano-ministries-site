The Last Convert

One man spent fifty years hunting the last soul on earth who had never heard the name of Jesus.

{{contact.first_name}},

I have written teaching books for you before. This one is different. This one is a novel, and I was not planning it. God put this story on my heart recently and told me to write it. So I wrote it.

Matthew 24:14 says the gospel will be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations, and then the end shall come. Ezra Whitfield read that verse as a young man and decided somebody ought to go find the last one. So he went.

The Story

He chased that soul to the ends of the earth. Up the Sepik in a dugout canoe. Into a Libyan cell. Across a hundred closed borders, into the far hills where no outsider had ever carried the gospel. He led a hundred thousand people to the Lord. He gave everything he had.

But the last convert was not who Ezra thought.

It races across the world to an ending that will stop you cold, and send you looking at your own life with new and urgent eyes. I will not say more than that. I do not want to spoil it for you.
