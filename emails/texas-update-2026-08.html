We just came home from Houston and Lufkin, Texas, and I have to tell you what God did.

The meetings were powerful. But the part I keep thinking about is the training. Christina taught a soul winning class on Saturday morning, and then we did not just talk about it. We took the class out and put it to work.

With a small team, we won over thirty people to the Lord.

Thirty souls. And here is the part that will outlast the trip: many of those believers are now on fire for winning the lost themselves. They went out nervous and they came back changed. That is what training does. You do not just reach people once, you leave behind people who keep reaching.
