Checking in from the road. We just finished three days at Church of Glory in Warsaw, and we are still carrying what happened in those rooms.

Bulgaria  •  September 18 to 21

Five services, start to finish

Night One  •  September 18

I Already Came. Now You Need to Go.

The disciples got the fire in the upper room and then they left. We went back into the upper room and stayed there singing come Jesus come, while Jesus has already come and is waiting on us. The person nobody else will ever reach is the one at the desk next to yours.

Watch Night One

Christina  •  September 19

Has Anyone Ever Told You That God Loves You?

Christina taught the room how to lead a stranger to Christ in about ninety seconds. Two questions, a short prayer, and a script anyone can memorize. She does not open with hell, because nobody wants to hear that and they already think it. She opens with love.

Since 2018 our family has recorded more than thirteen thousand people saying yes to Jesus with this. If you have ever wanted to witness and did not know what to say, watch this one.

Watch Christina Teach

Night Two  •  September 19

You Can't Count the Tomatoes Inside the Seed

Seed time and harvest. I told them about Reno's tomatoes, my grandfather's seeds dried on a board and forgotten on a shelf for fifteen years, and what came up out of one of them. And I told them about Rome, the broken window, the four thousand dollars gone, and the twenty eight thousand that came back. Exactly sevenfold. Not a penny more.

Watch Night Two

Sunday Service  •  September 20

Break the Yoke

Christina preached first, on the anointing that takes religion off a person, then fear, then worry. She told about a young man at a birthday party she was supposed to pray for and did not, and what she found out three months later. Then I taught communion, and we finished on the lifeboats.

Watch Sunday Service

Final Night  •  September 21

You Can Have Faith and Still Have No Power

The night before, the power went out in my hotel room. I could hear the refrigerator running, so I knew the electricity was there. I stepped into the hallway and every light was on. The man across the hall had power. I did not. There was a card that went in a slot on the wall, and that was the whole difference.

That is the church. The power is in the building. Other people plainly have it. And there is a step some of us have never taken.
