Ev. Merlin Calero

▶  Watch on YouTube

Prefer audio? Listen on Spotify

Merlin had two restaurants, a house, and three kids when God called him to Bible school. His family told him he was crazy. His wife's family told him he was crazy. His own pastor said, "You are one of my biggest givers, and now you are leaving."

They went anyway. Out of a four bedroom house into a two bedroom apartment with three children, for what they thought was one year. It took three, and he flew back and forth on weekends to keep the business alive while he sat in class.

He also came to this country from El Salvador as a boy, landed at LaGuardia on Christmas Eve, and saw snow for the first time. We talk about all of it, and about the night the Lord told him to sow the biggest seed of his life before he had any idea what it was for.
