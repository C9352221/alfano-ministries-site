A Special Report from Washington, D.C.
     

★ America 250 • 1776 – 2026 ★

It Felt Like America Again.

Five days in the nation's capital, celebrating America's 250th birthday.

Scenes from Washington, D.C.

Friend,

In 1976, I was born, the year America celebrated her 200th birthday. This year, our nation turned 250, and I had the honor of being right there in Washington, D.C. to celebrate it.

And I wasn't there alone. For the first time, I stood with three generations of my family at the same time, my son beside me, and my parents beside me. To carry the Gospel into the capital together, as a family, is a gift I will never forget.

What God Did

We Got to Serve Three Ministries

Three ministries that mean so much to us, all in five unforgettable days.

One • Soul Winning in the Streets

We hit the streets alongside Pastor Rodney Howard-Browne's teams, believing God for 250,000 decisions for Jesus. In just five days, we watched hundreds come to Christ right there on the streets of the capital.

Hundreds Saved

In Just 5 Days

Two • Intercessors for America

For three days we had the honor of serving Intercessors for America inside the Museum of the Bible, standing alongside my parents, Bill and Betsy West, and their amazing team of leaders.

We were blessed to meet Mike and Cindy Jacobs, Dutch Sheets, Danny Gokey, Coach Kennedy, and so many other movers in the body of Christ.

Three • Alfano Ministries International

And finally, we got to serve through Alfano Ministries International, where we led many to Jesus and made lasting friendships and connections that will bear fruit for years to come.

“The spirit of patriotism was strong at the celebration. For five days, it felt like America again.”

And here is the truth: none of what we do is possible without you, our amazing partner in the Gospel. Every soul, every seed, every open door, you are part of it. Thank you for standing with us.
