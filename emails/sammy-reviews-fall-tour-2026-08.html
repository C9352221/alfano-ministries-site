A Small Favor

Would You Leave a Review?

Sammy has only been on Amazon for one week. Right now, the single most powerful thing you can do for this ministry is leave an honest review. It costs you nothing but a few minutes, and it is what tells Amazon to start showing the book to parents and grandparents who have never heard of us. The first handful of reviews is what decides whether a new book gets seen at all.

If you have never left one before, here is exactly how:

1
 Go to the book's page on Amazon. The button below takes you straight there.
2
 Scroll down the page to the section called Customer reviews.
3
 Click the button that says Write a customer review. Sign in to your Amazon account if it asks.
4
 Tap the number of stars you want to give, from one to five.
5
 Add a short headline and two or three honest sentences. What did it change for you? Who would you hand it to? That is plenty. You can add a photo of the book if you like, which helps even more.
6
 Click Submit. That is it. It usually appears within a day or two.

One note: Amazon only accepts reviews from accounts that have spent at least $50 there in the past year. If yours will not let you post, please do not worry about it. A rating on Goodreads or simply telling a friend helps us just as much.
