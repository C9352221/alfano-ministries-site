Stop One  •  September 13

We started early in West Palm Beach

Before we ever got on the plane, we got to be at River Church in West Palm Beach on Sunday night. I shared a message on the offering, and it's one I keep coming back to.

Everybody wants the fire. Not everybody wants to make the offering. But there's no fire without one. I told them about the night we got robbed in Rome, the backpack out of the trunk with four thousand dollars and our passports in it, and what God did about it. Christina added it up months later. Twenty eight thousand dollars had come in. Exactly sevenfold. To the penny.

Watch the West Palm Beach Message

Istanbul, Turkey  •  Night 1

Revival week with the students

From Florida we flew to Istanbul to open the revival week for the students at the school of ministry. These are young people who left everything to learn how to carry the gospel, and they came hungry.

I preached on what revival actually costs. Green wood won't burn. Dead wood will. Revival has never once started because a preacher flew into town and rented a stadium. It starts in a room of hungry people who've emptied themselves out. That's how it started in the upper room, and that's how it still starts. We ended at the altar and the fire came.
Watch Istanbul Night 1

Istanbul  •  Night 2

We preached in the dark

Night two started with no power. None. No lights, no sound, no stream. We were off line and we preached in the dark.

Nobody left. Nobody moved. And about halfway through, the power came on. In the natural and in the supernatural at the same time.

The fire of God fell and many were touched. The devil can turn off a light switch. He can't stop what God is doing in a room full of hungry people.

New Episode  •  #67

He told God he'd never sell another drug

Tunji Adeyemi was bullied as a kid, so he became the one everybody else was scared of. That turned into drug dealing, and that turned into a debt he had no way to pay. He laid in his apartment staring at the ceiling and prayed, God, if You're real, clear this debt and I'll never sell another drug again.

He still can't tell you where the money came from. He paid every cent of it and he never sold again. Today he runs data center programs for Google all over the world. He'll tell you he doesn't have a ministry. Then he'll tell you about preaching inside maximum security prisons and juvenile detention, and teaching ten year olds on Sunday. Your job is the door. You are the ministry.
