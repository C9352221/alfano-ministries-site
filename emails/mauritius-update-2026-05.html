Mauritius Update

God is Doing Big Things.

We are just willing vessels.

Friend,

We have to share what God is doing on the ground in Mauritius. This is our first ever African nation, and from the very first night the Holy Spirit has been moving in ways we did not expect. Church after church. Outreach after outreach. Souls. Healings. Deliverance. The marketplace stirred to fund the Kingdom.

Here is where we are so far.

270

Souls Saved So Far

Many filled with the Holy Spirit. Many set free. Many healed and delivered.

A Divine Encounter

With Pastor Benny Hinn

The Lord crossed our path with Pastor Benny Hinn. A grace gift in the middle of this trip and a reminder that He goes before us.

Kingdom Business

The Marketplace is Stirred

Because of these meetings, the local business community has been motivated to start businesses and to give for the Kingdom. We saw it firsthand at our Kingdom Business Meeting. People are catching the vision that wealth follows obedience, and that obedience funds the Gospel.

New Doors

Three Continents Opening Up

Out of this trip, doors are flying open for Alfano Ministries.

  • Mauritius wants us back.
  • Europe keeps adding new ministry stops.
  • Madagascar is now an open invitation, thanks to the Prince of Madagascar himself, Rina Telesphore.

God is doing big things. We are just willing vessels.
