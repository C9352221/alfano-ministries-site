A Fifteen Second Prayer
Reached the Supreme Court

Coach Joe Kennedy and his wife Denise tell it themselves.

{{contact.first_name}},

When we were in Washington, D.C. this summer, we had the honor of meeting Coach Joe Kennedy. You may know the name. He is the high school football coach who knelt at the fifty yard line after games and prayed for about fifteen seconds. His school district told him to stop. He would not. It cost him his job, and it took seven years and the United States Supreme Court before he got it back.

This week, Coach Joe and his wife Denise sat down with me for over an hour on the podcast, and they held nothing back.

In This Episode

Joe's road from atheism to faith, and Denise's story of what those years did to their marriage and their community. The day the district came after him. What it is like to be the family everyone is watching. And the answer he gives when people ask if he would do it again.

“Your testimony is right there with the blood.”
