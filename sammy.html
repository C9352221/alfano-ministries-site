Meet Sammy

A Little Runt Who Was Chosen

Sammy was born the runt of his litter, way out in the woods of Washington, Pennsylvania. His whole house was an old tipped-over barrel. He had no soft bed, no warm bowl, no name, and nobody who loved him.

Then one day a family found him, chose him, and carried him home. They gave him a bowl of his own, a blue bandana, and a name. For the next fifteen years, Sammy traveled the country with his family, a very good dog with a very good life.

His story is one of belonging: the one nobody wanted became the one who was loved most. And he always said God was just "dog" spelled backwards.
